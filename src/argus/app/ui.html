How this is scored
Roughly two thirds of Form 4 rows are not decisions. Tax withholding (F), grants (A) and option mechanics (M) are payroll, and score exactly zero here — not a low score, zero, with the reason attached.

The 10b5-1 flag is decisive. A sale under a flagged plan was scheduled when the plan was adopted, so its timing carries no information.

Buying and selling are not symmetric. Open-market purchases (code P) are the best-documented effect in this literature. Sales are capped well below purchases because liquidity, tax and diversification explain most of them.

Every score decomposes. Each factor below a row shows what it added and why. Nothing here is a number you have to take on trust.

Settings

Argus reads public regulatory filings. The SEC requires anyone doing that to say who they are — a name and an email address. It is their fair-access rule, and without it every request is refused outright.

Professional status multiplies exchange data fees several-fold. It is asked rather than assumed because it is the largest cost variable in this system.

Secrets are not stored here. This file is plaintext JSON on your disk. The Claude API key is read from the ANTHROPIC_API_KEY environment variable at the moment it is used and is never written to disk — proper key isolation is a build step that does not exist yet, and pretending otherwise would be worse than saying so.
