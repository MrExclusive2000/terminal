How this is scored
Roughly two thirds of Form 4 rows are not decisions. Tax withholding (F), grants (A) and option mechanics (M) are payroll, and score exactly zero here — not a low score, zero, with the reason attached.

The 10b5-1 flag is decisive. A sale under a flagged plan was scheduled when the plan was adopted, so its timing carries no information.

Buying and selling are not symmetric. Open-market purchases (code P) are the best-documented effect in this literature. Sales are capped well below purchases because liquidity, tax and diversification explain most of them.

Every score decomposes. Each factor below a row shows what it added and why. Nothing here is a number you have to take on trust.

Settings

Data — SEC EDGAR

Argus reads public regulatory filings. The SEC requires anyone doing that to say who they are — a name and an email address. Without it every request is refused outright.

Broker — MetaTrader 5

Read-only. Argus reads prices and symbol metadata; there is no order path in the codebase. Leave the path blank to attach to whichever terminal is already running — which is the normal case.

IC Markets serves the same instrument as XAUUSD, XAUUSD.a or XAUUSD.r depending on account type. The wrong suffix looks like “symbol not found” rather than a settings problem, so Test detects it for you.

How to read this
This is not a signal. It is a lean, a strength, and the evidence that produced them — every line argues its own case and you can disagree with any of them.

Disagreement is shown, not netted away. Two strong opposing reads and two weak aligned ones produce the same average. They are not the same situation, so conflict is reported separately and caps the strength.

Confidence needs evidence, not just agreement. One input agreeing with itself is not conviction, so a thin board says so.

Every input carries its own age. Positioning is days old by law. Nothing here blends a stale number into a live one.
